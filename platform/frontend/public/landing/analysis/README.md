# Example studies

The ten images the landing page's feature grid cycles through (the "See" cell) and, for four of
them, the demo's studies. They are also what `platform/api/static/imaging-demo/` bundles.

## Licences: not all recorded

- `ct-brain.webp` and `pathology-2.webp` are CC0 / public domain (Wikimedia Commons).
- Eight were added or replaced on 2026-10-19 by ones the maintainer supplied: `xray`, `xray-2`,
  `xray-3`, `xray-4`, `ultrasound`, `ultrasound-2`, `mri-brain` and `pathology`. **Their source
  and licence have not been recorded yet.** Record them here, or swap them for CC0 images,
  before this is treated as clean.
- Never the RSNA/Kaggle samples under `real_data/`: their licence is academic and
  non-commercial and explicitly forbids redistribution.
- Never real patient studies.
